## 🌐 Product Vision

Modern software engineering careers suffer from an invisible disconnect: **what engineers build on a day-to-day basis is rarely reflected in their career velocity, resume impact, or promotion assessments.** 

Engineers write complex microservices, resolve concurrency bottlenecks, and scale database schemas, yet struggle to:
- Quantify their achievements into recruiter-ready metrics.
- Identify the exact technical blind spots holding them back from Staff/Lead tiers.
- Present a cohesive, living public portfolio that showcases real verified milestones instead of stale static links.

**CodeOrbit** solves this by unifying **AI-driven ATS resume intelligence**, **career roadmap milestones**, **skill proficiency benchmarking**, and an **aggregated dynamic portfolio builder** into a single, high-velocity engineering cockpit.

## 🚀 What CodeOrbit Provides to Users

### 1. 📄 Resume Upload & ATS Intelligence Parser (Powered by Gemini)
A comprehensive document scanner and career analysis engine:
- **Universal Resume Ingestion**: Effortlessly upload PDF (`.pdf`), Word (`.docx`), or plain text (`.txt`) resumes via an intuitive drag-and-drop dropzone or direct file picker.
- **Instant Verified Sample Demos**: Test immediately with pre-loaded, production-tested *Staff Full Stack Engineer* and *Cloud Native Platform Lead* benchmark profiles.
- **Gemini ATS Evaluation**: Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with structured extraction, paired with an engineering heuristic parser.
- **Candidate Scorecard & Circular Match Gauge**: Provides an overall score (e.g. `94/100`) accompanied by a 4-pillar dimensional breakdown:
  - **Keyword & Technical Stack Match**: Measures alignment with modern production toolchains.
  - **Quantifiable Project Metrics**: Verifies the presence of measurable impact statements (latency, QPS, uptime, cost reduction).
  - **System Architecture Depth**: Evaluates distributed systems, data modeling, and fault tolerance competencies.
  - **Seniority & Leadership Scope**: Measures readiness for L5 Senior and L6 Staff responsibilities.
- **Actionable Gap Detection**: Identifies critical missing competencies (e.g., *Kafka Idempotency*, *Envoy Service Mesh*, *Distributed Caching*, *Architecture Decision Records*) with clear rationales and step-by-step resolution actions.
- **1-Click Profile Auto-Population**:
  - Automatically merges generated quarterly milestones into your **Career Roadmap**.
  - Automatically imports extracted competencies and targeted learning gaps into your **Skill Tracker**.
  - One-click **"Populate Everything"** button that synchronizes your entire career profile in seconds.

---

### 2. 💼 Dynamic Portfolio Builder & Public Showcase
A living developer portfolio that builds itself as you hit engineering milestones:
- **Automated Roadmap Proof Points Aggregation**: Dynamically pulls completed quarterly roadmap deliverables and verified engineering accomplishments into your portfolio sheet.
- **Real-Time Skill Mastery Radar**: Aggregates tracked competencies, calculated mastery percentages, and category distributions into interactive proficiency bars.
- **Production GitHub Repositories**: Aggregates open-source tools, distributed systems, star counts, live system links, and architectural tags.
- **Interactive Portfolio Customizer Studio**:
  - **Curated Theme Presets**: Switch instantly between *Cyber Indigo*, *Emerald Aurora*, *Solar Amber*, and *Nebula Crimson*.
  - **Profile Customization**: Live-edit professional headlines, executive bio summaries, location status, and availability flags.
  - **Section Visibility Toggles**: Granular control over displaying proof points, skills radar, GitHub repositories, and contact modules.
  - **Dual Mode Views**: Seamlessly toggle between the *Studio Editor* and the *Live Interactive Preview*.
- **Public Shareable Link (`/portfolio/public`)**:
  - A clean public URL accessible to recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators without requiring an account.
  - One-click clipboard link sharing with toast confirmation.
  - Built-in **"Connect / Hire Me"** inquiry modal for direct recruiter communication.

- **Dedicated Print Optimization**: Custom `@media print` vector rendering engine that cleanses navigation sidebars, headers, and floating chrome.
- **Clean Vector Formatting**: Generates crisp, single/multi-page developer portfolios structured for applicant tracking systems and human executive review.
- **Zero Watermarks & Zero Clipping**: Guarantees accurate page margins, professional typography, and high-resolution layout.
